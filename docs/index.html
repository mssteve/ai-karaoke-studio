03 / FIND YOUR FLOW

From your library.
To your lead vocal.

  1. 01

    Bring a song.

    Choose Add songs, select an MP3 or MP4, then select your song in the library.

    ADD SONGS / MP3 / MP4
  2. 02

    Let local AI listen.

    Choose Extract with local AI to create four audio versions and synchronized lyrics. Models may download initially, and processing takes time.

    SEPARATE / TRANSCRIBE
  3. 03

    Take the lead.

    Choose Original, Enhanced, Vocal, or Instrumental, then press Play. Follow the lyrics and make the session yours.

    CHOOSE A MIX / PLAY / SING

Line up the next song. Use New playlist to make a set. A song’s Add to playlist menu adds it to a playlist or the persistent Queue.

Make the words your own. In the lyrics panel, choose Edit, enter plain text or LRC, then choose Update. Your saved wording is preserved.

04 / LOCAL BY DESIGN

Big studio energy.
Personal space.

Your music doesn’t need a round trip to the cloud. Audio separation and lyric recognition run locally on your PC, with FFmpeg, Demucs ONNX, and Whisper ONNX doing the heavy lifting.

Your audio stays on your machine. AI models may need to be downloaded before processing. Transcription results can vary; the lyric editor keeps you in control.
